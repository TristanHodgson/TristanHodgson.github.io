I am an Oxford mathematics student primarily interested in numerical analysis, scientific computing, and computational mathematics. My projects usually involve building mathematical models, testing them numerically, then trying to understand where things break down.

I have recently finished a research internship at Lancaster University’s Statistics and Operational Research Centre, studying models of redundant systems using Markov decision processes. You can find the project here.

I am now investigating how low-rank approximations of transformer weight matrices affect performance on simple cipher decryption tasks. The project can be found here.

Just a Pile of Links

  • The Problem with Parity of Esteem by Daisy Christodoulou (No More Marking) An excellent article discussing the (in hindsight) obvious differences between lower attaining students and those best suited to more practical study.
  • The People Do Not Yearn for Automation by Nilay Patel (The Verge) While I'm generally pro-ML, this video shows some very interesting blind spots of those who consider AI to be a universal good.
  • How our brightest minds get trapped in the City by Simon van Teutem (Financial Times) An excellent article discussing how ambition and life style inflation lead to so many intelligent people ending up trapped in the city.
  • James Martin on the Numbers Round of Countdown An excellent showing on countdown from my second year probability lecturer.
  • Try to Watch This Without Getting Angry by CGP Grey This video could, perhaps, be aptly titled as On the Origin of Ideas for the way in which it applies ideas of evolution to the propagation of ideas.
  • This video has n views by Tom Scott An excellent video about the impermanence of everything through the history of Web 2.0. Unfortunately, I have not written the code to copy the auto-updating view count.
  • Why city flags may be the worst-designed thing you've never noticed by Roman Mars (TED) This is my favourite TED talk. More generally, I love videos that show me a part of the world I had never considered; this video does so beautifully.
